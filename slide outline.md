# Why Are We Still Inventing Ships?

## Bio

Gabriel D. Weymouth is Professor of Ship Hydromechanics at Delft University of Technology. His research on fluid dynamics and ships combines mathematics, computation and machine learning to understand and optimize how ships move through water quickly and efficiently.

## Presentation Summary

Humans have been building ships for tens of thousands of years. So why are we still studying how they move through water and designing new ones?

The answer is that the physics of fluids is complicated, and there is still a great deal we cannot predict. Today, new computer simulations and machine learning are giving us new ways to understand and design ships. At the same time, autonomous technology and inspiration from nature are beginning to change what a ship can be.

This talk will look at how we predict the behavior of ships today, and then ask a more interesting question: *what might we call a ship in the future?*
